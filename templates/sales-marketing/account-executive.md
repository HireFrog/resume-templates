# Account Executive Resume Template

> **Format:** Markdown (`.md`) · **Designed for:** Humans + AI agents

*For B2B or B2C sales professionals who own the full deal cycle — from qualified opportunity to closed-won — across SMB, mid-market, or enterprise segments.*

Account Executive resumes are judged almost entirely on numbers: quota attainment, pipeline generated, deal size, and win rate. Hiring managers skim for evidence that you can carry a number and consistently hit it, so the resume needs to read like a track record of results rather than a list of duties. This template is built around quantified proof of sales performance, with room to show how you sell as well as what you closed.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name and contact info; include a LinkedIn URL since sales managers often verify social proof and endorsements.
2. **Summary** — 2-3 lines stating your segment (SMB/mid-market/enterprise), quota size, and top-line attainment record.
3. **Core Skills** — sales methodology, tools, and deal types so the resume clears keyword screens.
4. **Professional Experience** — reverse-chronological, each role built around quota, pipeline, and closed-revenue metrics.
5. **Awards & Recognition** — President's Club, Rookie of the Year, or similar; these carry real signal in sales hiring.
6. **Education** — degree and institution; sales roles rarely require it, so keep this brief.

## Key Competency Areas

- Consistent quota attainment across multiple periods, not just a single standout quarter
- Full-cycle deal ownership: prospecting, discovery, demo, negotiation, and close
- Pipeline generation and management, including self-sourced vs. inbound/SDR-sourced splits
- Deal size and average contract value, especially growth over time
- Sales methodology fluency (MEDDIC, Challenger, SPIN, Sandler, or similar)
- Forecasting accuracy and CRM discipline
- Cross-functional collaboration with SDRs, Customer Success, and Solutions Engineering
- Negotiation and objection handling in competitive or multi-stakeholder deals
- Account planning and territory prioritization within an assigned book or vertical

## Bullet Point Framework

Every bullet should follow **Action + Scope + Measurable Impact**: what you did, over what territory or deal size, resulting in what number. Lead with the verb, name the mechanism (segment, product line, deal type), and close with a metric — attainment percentage, dollar value, or rate.

- Closed [X% of quota] against a [$Y annual] target by managing a full-cycle pipeline of [Z] mid-market accounts
- Generated [$X in self-sourced pipeline] through outbound prospecting, contributing [Y%] of total book of business
- Grew average deal size from [$X to $Y] over [Z months] by shifting focus to multi-year enterprise contracts
- Achieved [X% win rate] against competitive alternatives by leading a MEDDIC-based qualification process
- Ranked [top X%] of [Y]-person sales team for [quarter/year], earning President's Club recognition
- Reduced average sales cycle from [X to Y days] by implementing a mutual close plan with prospects
- Expanded [X existing accounts] with add-on/upsell revenue totaling [$Y], contributing [Z%] of total quota

## Tips for Account Executive Resumes

- Lead every role with your quota and attainment number — it's the first thing sales managers look for.
- Distinguish self-sourced pipeline from inbound or SDR-sourced pipeline; hunters and farmers are evaluated differently.
- Name your sales methodology if you use one formally — it signals process discipline, not just talent.
- Quantify deal complexity (number of stakeholders, contract length, deal size) for enterprise roles where cycle length matters.
- Include rankings ("top 10% of 50 reps") when available — relative performance is often more persuasive than raw numbers.
- Keep the resume to one page unless you have 10+ years of experience or are applying for a senior/strategic AE role.
- If you carry both new-business and expansion quota, break out how much of your number came from each — hiring managers evaluate hunters and farmers differently.
