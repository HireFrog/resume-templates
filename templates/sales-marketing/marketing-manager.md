# Marketing Manager Resume Template

> **Format:** Markdown (`.md`) · **Designed for:** Humans + AI agents

*For generalist marketing leaders who plan, execute, and measure cross-channel campaigns, often while managing a small team or specialist contributors.*

Marketing Manager is a broad title, so the resume needs to establish scope quickly: which channels you own, what budget you manage, and what business results your campaigns drove. This template is built to show range across campaign planning, budget stewardship, and team coordination while still grounding every claim in a measurable outcome — because "managed marketing" without numbers reads as junior regardless of actual seniority.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name and contact info, plus LinkedIn and a portfolio link if you have public campaign work.
2. **Summary** — 2-3 lines on channel scope, budget size, and a headline result (pipeline, revenue, or growth metric).
3. **Core Skills** — channels, tools, and platforms to pass keyword screens and show breadth.
4. **Professional Experience** — reverse-chronological, each role tied to campaign performance, budget management, and team leadership if applicable.
5. **Selected Campaigns** (optional) — 2-3 standout campaigns with a one-line result each, useful when a single role covered many initiatives.
6. **Education** — degree and institution.

## Key Competency Areas

- Cross-channel campaign planning and execution (paid, email, events, partnerships, social)
- Budget management and return on marketing spend (ROMI/ROAS)
- Lead generation and pipeline contribution, tied to sales-accepted or sales-qualified metrics
- Brand awareness and share-of-voice growth
- Marketing operations: campaign tracking, attribution, and reporting
- Team leadership or agency/vendor management
- Cross-functional alignment with Sales, Product, and Creative
- Marketing calendar and launch-cadence ownership
- A/B testing and iterative optimization of campaign creative and offers

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + Scope + Measurable Impact**: name the campaign or channel, state its budget or reach, and close with a lead, revenue, or efficiency metric. Marketing Manager bullets should show both what you executed and the business result it produced.

- Managed a [$X] annual marketing budget across [Y channels], generating [Z marketing-qualified leads]
- Launched an integrated campaign across [email, paid social, and events] that drove [X% increase] in pipeline contribution
- Reduced cost per lead from [$X to $Y] by reallocating spend toward higher-performing channels
- Grew email subscriber base by [X%] and increased open rates from [Y% to Z%] through segmentation and testing
- Led a team of [X marketers] delivering [Y campaigns/quarter], improving campaign velocity by [Z%]
- Increased brand awareness metrics (aided/unaided recall) by [X%] through a coordinated [PR/content/social] push
- Improved marketing-qualified-to-sales-qualified lead conversion from [X% to Y%] by refining lead scoring with Sales

## Tips for Marketing Manager Resumes

- Clarify your scope immediately — which channels, what budget, how many people or vendors you managed.
- Tie every campaign to a business metric (leads, pipeline, revenue, or cost efficiency), not just "launched" or "executed."
- If your role spanned many small campaigns, use a Selected Campaigns section to highlight the 2-3 that mattered most.
- Name specific tools (HubSpot, Marketo, GA4, Meta Ads Manager) — Marketing Manager postings are often keyword-screened for platform experience.
- Show budget ownership explicitly; managing spend is a key differentiator between coordinator- and manager-level roles.
- If you led people, separate leadership bullets from execution bullets so both are visible at a glance.
- Be specific about which channels you owned directly vs. which you coordinated through an agency or another team.
