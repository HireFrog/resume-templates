# Content Marketing Manager Resume Template

*For marketers who plan and produce content programs — blogs, SEO, guides, video, or newsletters — to drive organic traffic, engagement, and pipeline.*

Content Marketing Manager resumes need to prove that content isn't just being produced, but is working: driving traffic, ranking in search, engaging an audience, and contributing to pipeline. This template centers on those outcome metrics while leaving room to show the editorial and operational skill (content calendars, SEO strategy, cross-format production) that hiring managers use to judge whether you can run a content function, not just write.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name and contact info, plus LinkedIn and a portfolio or published-work link — content marketers are expected to show samples.
2. **Summary** — 2-3 lines on content scope (formats, channels), audience size, and a headline traffic or pipeline result.
3. **Core Skills** — SEO, content platforms, and production/editorial tools for keyword matching.
4. **Professional Experience** — reverse-chronological, each role anchored in traffic, engagement, and content-attributed pipeline metrics.
5. **Selected Content/Portfolio** (optional) — 2-3 standout pieces or programs with a one-line result each.
6. **Education** — degree and institution.

## Key Competency Areas

- Organic traffic growth and search ranking performance (keyword rankings, share of organic sessions)
- Content-attributed pipeline and conversion from content assets (gated guides, case studies, tools)
- Editorial calendar management and consistent publishing cadence across formats
- SEO strategy: keyword research, on-page optimization, and content refreshes
- Audience engagement metrics (time on page, newsletter open/click rates, social shares)
- Cross-format content production (blog, video, podcast, newsletter, gated assets)
- Freelancer/contractor management for scaled content production
- Collaboration with Demand Gen, Product Marketing, and SEO/Growth on content strategy
- Content repurposing and distribution to extend reach without added production cost

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + Scope + Measurable Impact**: name the content motion (publishing cadence, SEO push, format launch), state the scale (posts/month, audience size), and close with a traffic, engagement, or pipeline metric.

- Grew organic traffic from [X to Y monthly sessions] over [Z months] through a keyword-driven content strategy
- Published [X pieces/month] across blog, video, and newsletter, increasing average engagement time by [Y%]
- Ranked [X keywords] on page one of Google, driving [Y%] of total organic traffic to the site
- Launched a gated content program that generated [X leads/month] at a [Y%] conversion rate
- Grew newsletter subscriber base from [X to Y], with open rates of [Z%] against an industry average of [W%]
- Managed a team of [X writers/freelancers] to scale content output by [Y%] without increasing per-piece cost

## Tips for Content Marketing Manager Resumes

- Lead with traffic and pipeline outcomes, not publishing volume alone — "wrote 50 posts" is weaker than "grew organic traffic 3x."
- Include a portfolio or published-work link; content hiring managers expect to see actual samples.
- Show SEO fluency explicitly (keyword research, ranking results, technical on-page work) since it's often core to the role.
- If you manage freelancers or writers, quantify the scale of that operation — it signals you can run a content function, not just write.
- Distinguish top-of-funnel content (traffic, awareness) from bottom-of-funnel content (gated assets, conversion) in your bullets.
- Use specific numbers for traffic and leads rather than vague growth claims — reviewers discount unquantified "significant increase" language.
