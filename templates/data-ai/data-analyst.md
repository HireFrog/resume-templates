# Data Analyst Resume Template

> **Format:** Markdown (`.md`) · **Designed for:** Humans + AI agents

*For analysts who turn raw data into decisions through querying, reporting, and analysis.*

Data analyst hiring managers look for SQL fluency, clear communication of insights, and evidence that your analysis actually changed a decision. This template emphasizes business impact over tool lists.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, email, portfolio/GitHub if you have public analysis work.
2. **Summary** — 1-3 lines on your domain (e.g. product, marketing, finance analytics) and core tools.
3. **Skills** — SQL, BI tools, and languages, grouped by category.
4. **Experience** — reverse-chronological, weighted toward decisions your analysis influenced.
5. **Projects** (optional) — personal analysis projects with public write-ups or dashboards.
6. **Education** — degree, school, year (optional past early career).

## Key Competency Areas

- Writing efficient SQL against large or complex datasets
- Building dashboards and reports for non-technical stakeholders
- Translating a business question into an analysis plan
- Statistical fundamentals (significance, correlation vs. causation, sampling)
- Communicating findings clearly to people who won't read the query
- Data cleaning and validation before drawing conclusions
- Partnering with product, marketing, or finance teams on recurring analysis needs

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + What you analyzed + Decision or outcome it drove**. The strongest analyst bullets show a decision changed because of your work, not just that a report was produced.

- Analyzed [dataset/metric] to identify [finding], leading [team] to [decision/change]
- Built [dashboard] tracking [metric] for [stakeholder group], replacing [X hours/week] of manual reporting
- Ran [A/B test or cohort analysis] on [feature], informing the decision to [outcome]
- Identified [trend/anomaly] in [X% of data], preventing [cost/risk] before it escalated
- Automated [recurring report], reducing turnaround time from [X days] to [Y hours]
- Partnered with [team] to define [metric/KPI], now used to track [business goal]

## Tips for Data Analyst Resumes

- Lead with the decision your analysis influenced, not the report you produced — "led to X" beats "created a report on X."
- Name your SQL fluency level implicitly through the complexity of what you describe (joins, window functions, large datasets).
- Show at least one bullet where you translated an ambiguous business question into a concrete analysis.
- Mention the audience for your work (executives, product managers, marketing) — it signals communication range.
- Keep dashboards and automation bullets focused on time saved or decisions enabled, not just "built a dashboard."
- One page is standard for most experience levels in this field.
