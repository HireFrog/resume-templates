# HR / People Operations Manager Resume Template

*For candidates who run HR programs, systems, and processes — from onboarding to performance to compliance.*

People Operations resumes need to show two things at once: empathy for employees and rigor about systems and compliance. This template balances both, emphasizing measurable program outcomes — retention, time-to-fill, engagement — rather than just a list of HR duties. The stronger your resume shows business impact alongside employee experience, the more competitive you'll be for manager-level roles.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, phone, email, LinkedIn.
2. **Summary** — 2-3 lines on your HR focus (People Ops, HRBP, Total Rewards, etc.) and scope.
3. **Core Skills** — systems, programs, and compliance areas.
4. **Professional Experience** — reverse-chronological, with program-outcome bullets.
5. **Education** — degree, institution, year.
6. **Certifications** — SHRM-CP/SCP, PHR/SPHR.
7. **Additional Sections** (optional) — programs you built from scratch, DEI initiatives, or multi-state/international compliance experience.

## Key Competency Areas

- Onboarding and offboarding process design
- HRIS administration (Workday, BambooHR, Rippling, etc.)
- Performance management program design
- Compliance and employment law awareness
- Compensation and benefits administration
- Employee relations and engagement
- Reporting on headcount, attrition, and engagement metrics
- Recruiting operations and hiring process design

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + Program + People/Business Impact**: describe the HR program or process you built, and quantify its effect on employees or the business. Where possible, pair a people metric (retention, engagement, satisfaction) with a business metric (time saved, cost avoided, compliance risk reduced).

- Redesigned the onboarding program for [200+] new hires/year, improving 30-day retention by [12%]
- Implemented [Workday/BambooHR], migrating [500+] employee records with zero data loss
- Reduced time-to-fill for open roles from [45 days] to [28 days] by streamlining the interview process
- Managed benefits enrollment for [300] employees, achieving [98%] on-time completion
- Built a quarterly engagement survey process, increasing response rate from [50%] to [85%]
- Led compliance audit preparation across [3] states, resulting in zero findings

## Tips for HR / People Operations Manager Resumes

- Quantify program reach — number of employees, locations, or hires touched — even for "soft" programs like engagement or culture initiatives.
- Name your HRIS and payroll systems explicitly; many People Ops roles are filtered by specific platform experience.
- Show compliance awareness concretely (audits passed, multi-state experience, employment law areas) — it builds trust fast in this function.
- Balance program-building bullets with people-facing outcomes (retention, engagement, satisfaction) — both matter to hiring managers.
- If you've supported a company through a specific milestone (rapid hiring, RIF, acquisition), mention it — it signals resilience under pressure.
- Avoid listing every HR task you've touched; prioritize the programs you owned end-to-end over ones you only supported.
