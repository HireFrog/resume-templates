# Supply Chain / Operations Analyst Resume Template

> **Format:** Markdown (`.md`) · **Designed for:** Humans + AI agents

*For candidates analyzing and optimizing logistics, inventory, procurement, or fulfillment operations.*

Supply chain analyst roles are evaluated on how well you turn operational data into decisions that reduce cost, improve speed, or reduce risk. This template emphasizes throughput, cost, and accuracy metrics — the language supply chain hiring managers scan for first — along with the scale of what you managed, since that signals seniority as much as title does.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, phone, email, LinkedIn.
2. **Summary** — 2-3 lines on your supply chain focus (inventory, logistics, procurement, demand planning) and scope.
3. **Core Skills** — analytical tools and supply chain domains.
4. **Professional Experience** — reverse-chronological, with efficiency- and cost-driven bullets.
5. **Education** — degree, institution, year.
6. **Certifications** — APICS/ASCM CPIM, Six Sigma, Lean.
7. **Additional Sections** (optional) — major systems implementations, disruption response, or notable cost-savings initiatives.

## Key Competency Areas

- Demand forecasting and inventory planning
- Logistics and transportation cost analysis
- Vendor/supplier performance analysis
- Data analysis (Excel, SQL, WMS/ERP systems)
- Process improvement and Lean/Six Sigma methods
- Cross-functional coordination (procurement, warehouse, sales)
- KPI tracking (on-time delivery, fill rate, inventory turns)
- Risk and disruption response planning

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + Process/System + Cost or Efficiency Impact**: describe what you analyzed or improved, and quantify the cost, time, or accuracy result. Whenever possible, name the scale of what you were working with — SKU count, vendor count, or spend — so the reader can gauge the size of the operation you supported.

- Analyzed [inventory turnover across 200 SKUs], identifying [$300K] in excess stock and reducing carrying cost by [15%]
- Improved on-time delivery rate from [88%] to [97%] by redesigning [carrier selection process]
- Built a [demand forecasting model] that reduced stockouts by [22%] during peak season
- Led supplier performance analysis across [30 vendors], informing a renegotiation that saved [$150K] annually
- Streamlined [warehouse putaway process], increasing throughput by [18%] without adding headcount
- Reduced order-to-delivery cycle time from [6 days] to [3.5 days] through [route optimization]

## Tips for Supply Chain / Operations Analyst Resumes

- Lead with cost and efficiency numbers — dollars saved, cycle time reduced, fill rate improved. These are the metrics supply chain hiring managers scan for first.
- Name the specific systems you've used (WMS, ERP, TMS platforms) — supply chain roles are often filtered by tool experience.
- Show the scale of what you managed: SKU count, number of vendors, warehouse volume, or transportation spend.
- If you've worked through a disruption (supplier issue, demand spike, capacity constraint), describe how you responded — it's a strong signal of judgment under pressure.
- Distinguish planning/forecasting work from execution/logistics work if you've done both — they read as different specializations.
- Quantify accuracy alongside speed — forecast accuracy, order accuracy, or inventory accuracy is as important to this function as throughput.
