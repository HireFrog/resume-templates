# Accountant Resume Template

> **Format:** Markdown (`.md`) · **Designed for:** Humans + AI agents

*For candidates in general accounting, staff/senior accountant, or accounting specialist roles.*

Accounting resumes are evaluated on accuracy, compliance, and process ownership — the goal is to show you can be trusted with the books. This template emphasizes precision, systems experience, and the specific accounting functions you own, rather than generic descriptions of "financial responsibilities." Naming your exact scope (GL, AP, AR, close, reporting) upfront helps a reviewer place you correctly within seconds.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, phone, email, LinkedIn.
2. **Summary** — 2-3 lines on your accounting focus (AP/AR, general ledger, month-end close) and scope.
3. **Core Skills** — accounting systems, standards, and technical skills.
4. **Professional Experience** — reverse-chronological, with process- and accuracy-driven bullets.
5. **Education** — degree, institution, year.
6. **Certifications** — CPA (licensed or in progress), or relevant coursework status.
7. **Additional Sections** (optional) — audit history, systems implementations, or process improvements not covered in your role bullets.

## Key Competency Areas

- Month-end and year-end close
- General ledger reconciliation
- Accounts payable / accounts receivable
- Financial statement preparation
- GAAP compliance and internal controls
- Accounting systems (QuickBooks, NetSuite, SAP, Oracle)
- Audit support and documentation
- Journal entries and account reconciliations

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + Process + Accuracy/Timeliness Impact**: describe the accounting function you owned and quantify accuracy, speed, or compliance outcomes. Numbers that show precision — zero discrepancies, days to close, dollar volume reconciled — carry more weight in accounting than general productivity claims.

- Managed month-end close for [3] entities, consistently closing within [5 business days]
- Reconciled [$2M+] in monthly transactions across [12] general ledger accounts with [zero material discrepancies]
- Reduced accounts receivable aging over 90 days from [18%] to [6%] through improved collections process
- Prepared and reviewed [financial statements] for [quarterly board reporting]
- Supported [annual audit], resolving [100%] of auditor requests within deadline with no findings
- Implemented [a new AP workflow] in [NetSuite], reducing invoice processing time by [35%]

## Tips for Accountant Resumes

- State exactly which functions you own — GL, AP, AR, close, reporting. "Accounting duties" is too vague to differentiate you.
- Quantify accuracy and timeliness, not just volume — "closed within 5 days with zero discrepancies" is stronger than "handled month-end close."
- List every major accounting system you've used by name; ATS filters for accounting roles frequently screen on specific software.
- If you're pursuing or hold a CPA, put it near the top of the resume — it's often a hard filter for mid-to-senior roles.
- Mention audit experience explicitly if you have it; it signals your work holds up under external scrutiny.
- If you've operated under multi-entity or multi-currency accounting, note it — it signals readiness for more complex environments.
