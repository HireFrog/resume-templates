# Product Manager Resume Template

> **Format:** Markdown (`.md`) · **Designed for:** Humans + AI agents

*For PMs who own a product area end to end — discovery, roadmap, execution, and outcomes.*

Product manager hiring managers look for evidence you can identify the right problem, ship a solution, and move a metric — not just coordinate a launch. This template emphasizes outcomes over activity.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, email, portfolio/case studies if you have them.
2. **Summary** — 1-3 lines on your domain (e.g. consumer growth, B2B platform) and the scale of product you've owned.
3. **Skills** — methodologies, tools, and technical fluency, grouped by category.
4. **Experience** — reverse-chronological, weighted toward metrics moved and decisions made.
5. **Selected Launches** (optional) — standout launches worth a dedicated line if they don't fit cleanly into a role bullet.
6. **Education** — degree, school, year (optional past early career).

## Key Competency Areas

- Identifying and prioritizing the right problems to solve
- Running discovery: user research, data analysis, competitive landscape
- Writing clear specs/PRDs and aligning engineering and design around them
- Shipping features and measuring their impact against a defined metric
- Prioritization and roadmap tradeoffs under limited engineering capacity
- Communicating product decisions and rationale to stakeholders and leadership
- Working cross-functionally with engineering, design, and go-to-market teams

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + Product area/feature + Measurable outcome**. Strong PM bullets connect a shipped feature to a metric that moved, not just a launch date.

- Launched [feature] for [product area], increasing [metric, e.g. activation, retention] by [X%]
- Led discovery for [problem area], identifying [insight] that reshaped the roadmap for [quarter/team]
- Shipped [feature] in [timeframe], reaching [X users] and driving [$X revenue / X% engagement]
- Prioritized [roadmap] across [X competing asks], increasing team output/velocity by [X%]
- Ran [experiment] on [feature], leading to a [X%] lift in [metric] and a full rollout
- Partnered with [team] to reduce [friction point], improving [funnel metric] by [X%]

## Tips for Product Manager Resumes

- Lead with the metric that moved, not the feature that shipped — "increased activation by 12%" beats "launched onboarding flow."
- Show your role in discovery, not just execution — PM resumes that only list shipped features read as project management, not product management.
- Name the size of your product area (users, revenue, team size) to establish scope.
- Include at least one bullet about a prioritization tradeoff you made and why.
- Avoid listing every feature you touched — pick the 4-6 with the clearest outcomes.
- One page is standard for most experience levels in this field.
