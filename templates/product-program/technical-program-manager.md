# Technical Program Manager Resume Template

> **Format:** Markdown (`.md`) · **Designed for:** Humans + AI agents

*For program managers driving execution on programs with significant engineering complexity and cross-org dependencies.*

TPM hiring managers look for evidence you can engage credibly with engineering on technical risk while still driving disciplined execution across teams. This template combines technical fluency with program delivery rigor.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, email, GitHub if relevant.
2. **Summary** — 1-3 lines on your technical domain and the scale of programs you've run.
3. **Skills** — technical fluency, program management methods, and tools.
4. **Experience** — reverse-chronological, weighted toward technical risk management and delivery outcomes.
5. **Selected Programs** (optional) — standout technical programs.
6. **Education** — degree, school, year (optional past early career).

## Key Competency Areas

- Managing complex, multi-team technical programs (migrations, platform builds, infra rollouts)
- Understanding technical architecture well enough to identify real risk, not just schedule risk
- Coordinating across engineering, infra, security, and product teams
- Running technical risk reviews and driving mitigation before issues surface
- Communicating technical tradeoffs and program status to both engineers and executives
- Managing dependencies across teams with different priorities and release cadences
- Driving process improvements for how technical programs are planned and tracked

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + Technical program/scope + Delivery or risk outcome**. TPM bullets should show both technical risk judgment and delivery discipline.

- Led [technical migration/program] across [X teams], delivering [outcome] with zero [downtime/data loss]
- Identified [technical risk] early in [program], avoiding [estimated cost/delay] by [mitigation taken]
- Coordinated [X engineering teams] through [platform rollout], hitting [X of X] milestones on schedule
- Drove [architecture/infra decision] alignment across [X teams] with competing priorities
- Built technical risk review process for [program type], reducing scope surprises by [X%]
- Managed [$X budget / X engineers] program delivering [technical outcome] in [timeframe]

## Tips for Technical Program Manager Resumes

- Show technical risk judgment explicitly — a bullet about catching a real architectural risk early is a strong TPM signal.
- If you have an engineering background, mention it briefly; it's a credibility signal for this role.
- Quantify delivery outcomes (on-time %, zero-downtime migrations, budget adherence) alongside technical scope.
- Name the specific technical domains involved (migrations, infra, platform builds) rather than generic "cross-functional programs."
- Distinguish your role from a TPM-adjacent engineering manager or product manager clearly in your summary.
- One page is standard for most experience levels.
