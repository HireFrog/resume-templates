# Technical Product Manager Resume Template

> **Format:** Markdown (`.md`) · **Designed for:** Humans + AI agents

*For PMs who own products with significant technical depth — APIs, platforms, infrastructure, or developer-facing tools.*

Technical PM hiring managers look for evidence you can engage credibly with engineering on architecture and tradeoffs, not just translate requirements. This template emphasizes technical fluency alongside product outcomes.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, email, GitHub/portfolio if relevant.
2. **Summary** — 1-3 lines on your technical domain (e.g. APIs, data platforms, infra) and product outcomes.
3. **Skills** — technical fluency, methodologies, and tools.
4. **Experience** — reverse-chronological, weighted toward technical decisions and product outcomes together.
5. **Selected Projects** (optional) — technical platforms or APIs you've owned.
6. **Education** — degree, school, year (optional past early career).

## Key Competency Areas

- Owning technical products: APIs, platforms, internal tools, or developer-facing products
- Engaging credibly with engineering on architecture tradeoffs and technical debt
- Translating technical constraints into product and business tradeoffs for stakeholders
- Writing technical specs and API contracts alongside standard PRDs
- Balancing technical investment (scalability, reliability) against feature velocity
- Understanding system design well enough to make informed prioritization calls
- Partnering closely with engineering leads as a technical peer, not just a requirements source

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + Technical product/system + Business or technical outcome**. Technical PM bullets should show both product judgment and real technical engagement.

- Owned [API/platform], driving adoption from [X to Y integrations/developers]
- Led technical strategy for [system], balancing [scalability investment] against [feature roadmap], improving [metric] by [X%]
- Partnered with engineering to redesign [architecture/API], reducing integration time for partners by [X%]
- Prioritized [technical debt/reliability work] against feature requests, reducing [incident rate] by [X%]
- Defined API contract for [product], adopted by [X internal/external teams]
- Shipped [technical feature] requiring deep system understanding, resulting in [$X impact / X% improvement]

## Tips for Technical Product Manager Resumes

- Show real technical engagement — architecture discussions, API design, tradeoff decisions — not just "worked closely with engineering."
- If you have an engineering or technical background, mention it briefly in the summary; it's a credibility signal for this role.
- Quantify both technical outcomes (integration time, reliability) and business outcomes (adoption, revenue) where you can.
- Name the specific systems or platforms you've owned rather than vague terms like "backend products."
- Include a bullet about a technical tradeoff you helped navigate — it demonstrates the technical judgment this role requires.
- One page is standard for most experience levels.
