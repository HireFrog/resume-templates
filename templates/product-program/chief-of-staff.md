# Chief of Staff Resume Template

> **Format:** Markdown (`.md`) · **Designed for:** Humans + AI agents

*For operators who extend an executive's leverage across strategy, operations, and cross-functional execution.*

Chief of Staff hiring is highly variable by company, but hiring managers consistently look for evidence of high judgment, discretion, and the ability to drive execution without formal authority. This template emphasizes leverage created for the executive and organization, not task completion.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, email.
2. **Summary** — 1-3 lines on the type of executive/org you've supported and the scope of your remit.
3. **Skills** — strategic, operational, and cross-functional capabilities.
4. **Experience** — reverse-chronological, weighted toward leverage created and initiatives driven.
5. **Selected Initiatives** (optional) — standout cross-functional projects.
6. **Education** — degree, school, year (optional past early career).

## Key Competency Areas

- Acting as a trusted extension of an executive across strategy and operations
- Driving cross-functional initiatives on the executive's behalf, often without formal authority
- Running executive operating rhythms: leadership meetings, OKRs, board prep
- Synthesizing information across the org into clear recommendations
- Managing sensitive or confidential initiatives with discretion
- Identifying organizational gaps and building the process or team to fill them
- Representing the executive's priorities credibly to other leaders

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + Initiative/scope + Leverage or business outcome**. Chief of Staff bullets should show what got done because you existed, not just what you attended.

- Led [cross-functional initiative] on behalf of [executive], delivering [outcome] across [X teams]
- Built and ran [operating rhythm, e.g. weekly leadership review], improving [decision speed/alignment] org-wide
- Managed [confidential initiative, e.g. reorg, fundraise prep], ensuring [outcome] with zero [leaks/disruption]
- Identified [organizational gap], building [process/team] that closed it within [timeframe]
- Synthesized input from [X stakeholders] into [recommendation], adopted by [executive/board]
- Drove [board prep/investor update] process, reducing prep time from [X days] to [Y days]

## Tips for Chief of Staff Resumes

- Show leverage, not tasks — a strong bullet reads as "built X that changed how leadership decided Y," not "supported the CEO with X."
- Quantify the scope of what you drove (teams involved, budget, org size) wherever you can.
- Include at least one bullet about discretion or judgment under sensitive circumstances, described without specifics that would breach confidentiality.
- Name the executive's title and company stage/size to establish context for the scope of the role.
- Avoid vague "trusted advisor" language without a concrete example backing it up.
- This role varies widely by company — tailor which competencies you emphasize based on whether the target role skews strategic, operational, or executive-support.
