# Project Manager Resume Template

*For project managers who plan, execute, and deliver defined projects on time and within budget.*

Project manager hiring managers look for evidence of structured planning, risk management, and consistent on-time, on-budget delivery. This template emphasizes concrete project outcomes over process descriptions.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, email.
2. **Summary** — 1-3 lines on the type of projects you manage (e.g. software delivery, construction, marketing campaigns) and their typical scale.
3. **Skills** — project management methodologies and tools.
4. **Experience** — reverse-chronological, weighted toward delivery outcomes.
5. **Certifications** (optional) — PMP, CAPM, PRINCE2, or similar if you have them.
6. **Education** — degree, school, year (optional past early career).

## Key Competency Areas

- Developing project plans, timelines, and budgets
- Managing scope, schedule, and budget tradeoffs (the project management triangle)
- Identifying and mitigating project risks before they cause delays
- Coordinating cross-functional or cross-vendor teams
- Running status reporting and stakeholder communication
- Managing change requests without derailing the original scope
- Closing out projects with clear documentation and lessons learned

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + Project scope + Delivery outcome**. Project manager bullets should show budget, timeline, and scope managed against a real constraint.

- Delivered [project] on time and [X%] under a [$X] budget, coordinating [X team members/vendors]
- Managed [X concurrent projects] with a combined budget of [$X], achieving [X%] on-time delivery rate
- Identified and mitigated [risk], preventing an estimated [X-week] delay to [project]
- Reduced project cycle time from [X months] to [Y months] through [process improvement]
- Led [X-person] cross-functional team through [project], delivering [outcome] despite [constraint]
- Managed change requests for [project], keeping scope creep under [X%] of original budget

## Tips for Project Manager Resumes

- Quantify budget, timeline, and team size for every major project you list — these establish scope quickly.
- Show on-time and on-budget delivery rates explicitly if you manage a portfolio of projects.
- Include at least one bullet about a risk you caught and mitigated before it caused a delay.
- List relevant certifications (PMP, PRINCE2, CAPM) prominently; they're a strong credibility signal in this field.
- Name the specific PM methodology and tools you use — many organizations look for fit with their existing process.
- One page is standard for most experience levels.
