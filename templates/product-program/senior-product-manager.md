# Senior Product Manager Resume Template

*For PMs who own a larger, more ambiguous product area and increasingly shape strategy, not just execution.*

At the senior level, resumes need to show strategic ownership — setting direction for a product area, not just shipping within one. This template is built around scope, ambiguity, and strategic influence.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, email, portfolio/case studies.
2. **Summary** — 2-3 lines on your domain, the scope you've owned, and the caliber of decisions you've driven.
3. **Skills** — methodologies, tools, and cross-functional leadership.
4. **Experience** — reverse-chronological, weighted toward strategy and org-level outcomes.
5. **Selected Initiatives** (optional) — major cross-functional or strategic efforts.
6. **Education** — degree, school, year (optional past early career).

## Key Competency Areas

- Setting product strategy for a domain, not just executing a given roadmap
- Making prioritization calls under significant ambiguity or competing stakeholder interests
- Leading cross-functional teams (design, engineering, data, go-to-market) toward a shared goal
- Mentoring junior PMs and raising product craft on the team
- Influencing company-level strategy through product-level insight
- Owning outcomes at the P&L or north-star-metric level, not just feature level
- Managing up: communicating tradeoffs and risk clearly to executive stakeholders

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **Action + Strategic scope + Business outcome**. Senior PM bullets should show you shaped the "what" and "why," not just the "how."

- Defined product strategy for [domain], driving [north-star metric] from [X] to [Y] over [timeframe]
- Led cross-functional team of [X] across [design/eng/data] to ship [initiative], resulting in [$X impact]
- Made the call to [sunset/pivot] [product/feature], reallocating [X engineers] to higher-leverage work
- Mentored [X] PMs, raising [team-level outcome, e.g. discovery rigor, launch quality]
- Presented [strategy/business case] to executive leadership, securing [$X / X headcount] investment
- Owned [P&L/metric] for [product area], improving it by [X%] over [timeframe]

## Tips for Senior Product Manager Resumes

- Show strategic ownership explicitly — "defined strategy for X" reads stronger than "managed roadmap for X."
- Quantify the north-star or business metric your area is judged on, and show movement on it over time.
- Include a bullet about a hard call you made (sunsetting a feature, reallocating resources) — it signals judgment.
- Name the size and composition of teams you led cross-functionally.
- Reference executive-level communication explicitly if you've presented strategy or business cases upward.
- One page is still achievable for most; two pages only if every line earns its place.
