# Senior Software Engineer Resume Template

> **Format:** Markdown (`.md`) · **Designed for:** Humans + AI agents

*For engineers who own significant systems end-to-end and are expected to work with less oversight.*

At the senior level, hiring managers look past "can you code" toward "can you own ambiguous problems, make sound technical tradeoffs, and raise the bar for the people around you." This template is built to surface ownership, technical judgment, and cross-team influence rather than a longer list of tasks completed. Every bullet should answer "what did you own, and what changed because you owned it."

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, email, GitHub/portfolio, LinkedIn.
2. **Summary** — 2-3 lines on your domain expertise, scope of ownership, and leadership scope.
3. **Skills** — languages, frameworks, and systems/architecture areas, grouped for scanability.
4. **Experience** — reverse-chronological, with ownership- and impact-driven bullets weighted toward the last 5-7 years.
5. **Selected Projects** (optional) — 1-2 significant systems you designed or led that don't fit neatly into a job bullet.
6. **Certifications** (optional) — include only if directly relevant to the role (cloud, security, architecture).
7. **Education** — degree, institution, year; brief, since experience now carries the resume.

## Key Competency Areas

- End-to-end ownership of systems or services, including their long-term health
- Technical design and architecture tradeoffs under real constraints
- Mentoring and raising the technical bar for engineers around you
- Cross-team technical collaboration and dependency management
- Incident response and production reliability for systems you own
- Driving technical decisions and unblocking ambiguous problems with limited oversight
- Estimating and scoping work realistically, including calling out risk early
- Balancing short-term delivery pressure against long-term system health
- Writing design docs that hold up to scrutiny from other senior engineers

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **action + system or decision owned + business or technical impact**. Name what you owned or designed at a scope larger than a single task, and quantify reliability, performance, or team-level impact rather than just describing the work.

- Designed and led the build of [a payments reconciliation service] processing [$50M/month], reducing discrepancies by [90%]
- Owned [the checkout service] end-to-end, improving uptime from [99.5%] to [99.95%]
- Led the migration of [a monolith component] to [microservices], cutting deploy time from [45min] to [8min]
- Mentored [4] engineers, [2] of whom were promoted within [12 months]
- Drove the technical design for [a cross-team initiative] spanning [3 teams] and [6 months]
- Reduced [production incidents] by [50%] by introducing [better observability/testing practices]

## Tips for Senior Software Engineer Resumes

- Emphasize ownership boundaries — what system or domain was entirely yours to design and run.
- Show technical decision-making, not just execution: "chose X over Y because Z" is stronger signal than a list of technologies used.
- Include mentoring and cross-team influence explicitly; at this level it's evaluated alongside individual output.
- Quantify reliability metrics (uptime, incident rate, latency) if you own production systems — they carry more weight at senior level than feature counts.
- Cut early-career bullets that no longer represent your current scope; keep the resume focused on the last 5-7 years.
- If you've influenced hiring, architecture reviews, or on-call structure, mention it briefly — these are senior-level signals recruiters look for.
- Show at least one instance where you pushed back on a plan and the outcome was better for it — it demonstrates judgment, not just agreeableness.
