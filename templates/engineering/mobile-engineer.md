# Mobile Engineer Resume Template

> **Format:** Markdown (`.md`) · **Designed for:** Humans + AI agents

*For engineers building native or cross-platform iOS and Android applications.*

Mobile hiring managers want to see apps that shipped to real app stores, held up under real usage, and moved metrics that matter — crash rate, load time, ratings, retention. This template emphasizes platform depth and store-level outcomes over generic feature lists, since a mobile resume is one of the few kinds a hiring manager can go verify directly by downloading the app.

## Resume Structure

1. **Header** — name, location, email, GitHub, App Store/Play Store links if your apps are public.
2. **Summary** — 1-3 lines on your platform focus (iOS, Android, or cross-platform) and the kind of apps you've shipped.
3. **Skills** — languages, platform SDKs, and cross-platform frameworks, grouped by category.
4. **Experience** — reverse-chronological, weighted toward shipped releases and app-level metrics.
5. **Projects** — especially valuable if you have apps live on the App Store or Play Store.
6. **Education** — degree, school, year (optional past early career).

## Key Competency Areas

- Building and shipping production apps on iOS, Android, or both
- Working with native SDKs (UIKit/SwiftUI, Jetpack Compose/Android Views) or a cross-platform framework (React Native, Flutter)
- Managing app performance: startup time, memory, battery, crash rate
- Navigating app store submission, review, and release processes
- Handling offline support, background tasks, and platform-specific constraints
- Integrating with device APIs (camera, location, push notifications, biometrics)
- Writing and maintaining automated UI and unit tests for mobile
- Coordinating app releases with backend/API changes

## Bullet Point Framework

Use **action + what you shipped + app-level or store metric**. Mobile impact often shows up as crash rate, load time, store rating, or install/retention numbers — use whichever is honestly true and specific to what you actually owned.

- Shipped [feature] to [X users/downloads], improving [App Store/Play Store rating] from [X] to [Y]
- Reduced app crash rate from [X%] to [Y%] by [root-cause fix, e.g. memory leak, threading issue]
- Cut cold-start time by [X%] through [optimization, e.g. lazy loading, dependency reduction]
- Migrated [module] from [old framework, e.g. UIKit] to [new framework, e.g. SwiftUI], reducing [metric] by [X%]
- Led release of [app/feature] to [iOS/Android], reaching [X downloads] within [timeframe]
- Built offline support for [feature], reducing support tickets related to [issue] by [X%]

## Tips for Mobile Engineer Resumes

- Link directly to your apps on the App Store or Play Store when possible — a live listing with ratings is stronger proof than any bullet.
- State the platform explicitly (iOS, Android, or both) at the top — mobile hiring is often platform-specific.
- Quantify crash rate, load time, and store rating changes; these are the numbers mobile hiring managers scan for first.
- Mention app store submission and release experience if you've owned it — many candidates have only written code, not shipped it.
- Note experience with cross-platform frameworks separately from native SDK experience so reviewers can gauge fit precisely.
- Keep it to one page unless you have 8+ years or multiple flagship apps worth detailing.
