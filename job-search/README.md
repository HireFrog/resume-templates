# Job Search Guides

A short set of practical guides covering the core mechanics of a job search, from planning through follow-up. Read them in order, or jump to whichever stage you're in.

- **[01 - Job Search Strategy](01-job-search-strategy.md)** — How to plan your search: target role, timeboxing, and balancing volume with targeting.
- **[02 - Finding Jobs](02-finding-jobs.md)** — Where real openings live and how to evaluate whether a listing is worth your time.
- **[03 - Networking & Referrals](03-networking-referrals.md)** — Building and using a network to get warm introductions instead of cold applications.
- **[04 - Application Strategy](04-application-strategy.md)** — How to prioritize applications and tailor your materials without burning out.
- **[05 - Follow-Up](05-follow-up.md)** — How and when to follow up after applying and after interviews.
