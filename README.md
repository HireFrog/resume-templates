## How to use a template

1. Open the template closest to your target role.
2. Copy the skeleton in the `## Template` section into your editor of choice.
3. Fill in your real experience using the bullet-point framework as a guide — swap every bracketed placeholder for something true and specific.
4. Read the `## Tips` section for what actually matters for that role.

That gets you a solid, generic resume. The next step is making it not generic.

## Where HireFrog comes in

A template gives you a strong starting structure. It can't know your actual career history, and it definitely can't know what a specific employer, for a specific job, is actually looking for.

That's the gap **[HireFrog](https://www.hirefrog.co)** is built to close — tailoring your real experience to a specific job description, and helping you manage the rest of the job search from there: finding roles, prioritizing applications, tracking where things stand, and getting AI assistance along the way.
